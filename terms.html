A few simple notes about using Top Links.

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Top Links is a directory that helps you discover other websites. Please use the site and the websites it links to responsibly.

Using the directory

You can browse, search, and save favorites for personal use. Please do not interfere with the site, misuse feedback channels, or submit misleading or harmful links.

External websites

Links open services operated by other organizations. Those organizations set their own terms, privacy practices, prices, and availability. A listing does not imply a partnership or endorsement. Check the destination website before signing up, paying, downloading, or sharing information.

Availability and accuracy

We aim to keep the directory useful, but links, descriptions, and logos can become outdated. Listings may change or be removed. Open source project entries may require separate installation or hosting; not every listing is a ready-to-use online service.

Names, logos, and directory sources

Website names and logos identify their respective services and belong to their owners. Imported directory data has its own attribution and license, described in directory sources.

Your browser data

Favorites and preferences are stored locally in your browser. Clearing browser data can remove them. Read the privacy notice for details.

Questions or corrections
